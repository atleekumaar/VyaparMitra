# VyaparMitra Analytics Dictionary (Phase 2)

This document provides a comprehensive specification for every analytical metric produced by the **VyaparMitra Phase 2 Business Intelligence & Analytics Engine**.

---

## 1. Sales & Revenue Metrics

### Total Net Revenue
* **Definition**: Cumulative realized revenue after promotional discounts.
* **Formula**: $\sum \text{net\_amount} = \sum (\text{total\_amount} - \text{discount\_amount})$
* **Source**: `transaction_features.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Overall / Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Merchant
* **Example**: ₹15,510,039.34
* **Caveat**: Excludes failed, refunded, or quarantined transaction records.

### Total Order Count
* **Definition**: Total count of completed, valid customer orders.
* **Formula**: $\text{COUNT}(\text{transaction\_id})$
* **Source**: `transaction_features.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Overall / Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Merchant
* **Example**: 9,995 orders
* **Caveat**: Multi-item purchases under a single basket share one transaction ID.

### Average Order Value (AOV)
* **Definition**: Realized net revenue per transaction order.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Total Net Revenue}}{\text{Total Orders}}$
* **Source**: `transaction_features.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Merchant / Customer
* **Example**: ₹1,551.78
* **Caveat**: High-ticket outlier purchases (e.g. laptops) skew arithmetic mean upwards relative to median.

### Average Units Per Order
* **Definition**: Mean physical item count purchased per basket.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\sum \text{quantity}}{\text{Total Orders}}$
* **Source**: `transaction_features.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Merchant
* **Example**: 1.27 units
* **Caveat**: Does not differentiate unit pricing across heterogeneous categories.

### Discount Rate
* **Definition**: Proportion of gross sales value conceded to customer discounts.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\sum \text{discount\_amount}}{\sum \text{total\_amount}}$
* **Source**: `transaction_features.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Overall / Periodic / Merchant
* **Example**: 0.0521 (5.21%)
* **Caveat**: Calculated against gross catalog list price.

### Period-over-Period Growth Rate
* **Definition**: Percentage change in revenue or orders between contiguous time periods.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Metric}_t - \text{Metric}_{t-1}}{\text{Metric}_{t-1}} \times 100\%$
* **Source**: `sales_monthly.parquet`, `sales_weekly.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Weekly / Monthly
* **Example**: +8.42%
* **Caveat**: Subject to calendar day length variations (e.g. February 28 days vs January 31 days).

## 2. Customer & Behavioral Metrics

### Active Customers
* **Definition**: Unique count of customers who placed at least one completed transaction in the evaluation window.
* **Formula**: $\text{COUNT(DISTINCT } \text{customer\_id)}$
* **Source**: `customer_features.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Overall / Monthly / Merchant
* **Example**: 3,308 customers
* **Caveat**: Does not track non-purchasing store visitors.

### Repeat Customer Ratio
* **Definition**: Percentage of customer base with two or more historical transactions.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Count of customers with orders} > 1}{\text{Total Customers}} \times 100\%$
* **Source**: `customer_summary.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Overall / Merchant
* **Example**: 48.2%
* **Caveat**: Increases monotonically with longer observation windows.

### RFM Scores & Segments
* **Definition**: Quantile-based customer scoring (1 to 5) across Recency ($R$), Frequency ($F$), and Monetary Value ($M$).
* **Formula**:
  * $R = \text{Quantile}( \text{days since last order} )^{-1}$ (Lower recency days yields higher score 5)
  * $F = \text{Quantile}( \text{total order count} )$ (Higher orders yields higher score 5)
  * $M = \text{Quantile}( \text{total lifetime spend} )$ (Higher spend yields higher score 5)
* **Source**: `customer_segments.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Customer SKU
* **Segments Assigned**: Champions ($R \ge 4, F \ge 4, M \ge 4$), Loyal ($F \ge 3, M \ge 3$), Potential Loyalists ($R \ge 4, F \le 2, M \ge 2$), New Customers ($R \ge 4, F = 1$), At Risk ($R \le 2, F \ge 2$), Low Engagement (Remaining).
* **Example**: `C03276` -> R:5, F:4, M:5 -> Score: 545 -> Segment: Champions
* **Caveat**: Descriptive behavioral classification only; not a churn prediction or prescriptive recommendation.

## 3. Product & Category Metrics

### Realized Product Revenue Share
* **Definition**: Proportion of total merchant net revenue generated by an individual SKU or category.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Product / Category Net Revenue}}{\text{Total Platform Net Revenue}}$
* **Source**: `product_summary.parquet`, `category_summary.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: SKU / Category
* **Example**: Laptops -> 15.2% revenue share
* **Caveat**: Does not reflect gross profit margins or SKU holding carrying costs.

### Average Realized Price
* **Definition**: Effective realized unit revenue after discounts.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Product Net Revenue}}{\text{Product Physical Units Sold}}$
* **Source**: `product_summary.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: SKU
* **Example**: Leather Wallet -> ₹4,665.86
* **Caveat**: Varies based on promotional discounting applied at POS.

### Pareto Concentration Milestones
* **Definition**: Cumulative percentage of revenue contributed by top percentile brackets of SKUs.
* **Formula**: Cumulative sum of ranked SKU revenue divided by total revenue.
* **Source**: `data/analytics/products/product_rankings.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Catalog
* **Example**: Top 20% of SKUs represent 58.4% of total revenue.
* **Caveat**: Purely descriptive concentration metric; does not recommend dropping tail SKUs.

## 4. Contextual Metrics (Festivals & Weather)

### Festival Day Revenue Difference (%)
* **Definition**: Relative percentage difference between average daily revenue on festival dates versus non-festival dates.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Mean Daily Revenue}_{\text{Festival}} - \text{Mean Daily Revenue}_{\text{Non-Festival}}}{\text{Mean Daily Revenue}_{\text{Non-Festival}}} \times 100\%$
* **Source**: `festival_analysis.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Overall / Per-Festival Event
* **Example**: -10.96%
* **Caveat**: Observational comparison; festivals falling on weekdays versus weekends influence baseline volume.

### Rainy Day Revenue Uplift (%)
* **Definition**: Relative percentage difference between average daily revenue on rainy days ($> 0\text{ mm}$ precipitation) versus dry days.
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Mean Daily Revenue}_{\text{Rainy}} - \text{Mean Daily Revenue}_{\text{Dry}}}{\text{Mean Daily Revenue}_{\text{Dry}}} \times 100\%$
* **Source**: `weather_analysis.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Overall / City
* **Example**: +37.25%
* **Caveat**: Observational association; does not imply precipitation causes demand increases.

## 5. Trend & Anomaly Metrics

### Trend Classification
* **Definition**: Directional classification of rolling metric trajectory.
* **Formula**:
  * $\text{Increasing}$: Period-over-period percentage change $> +3.0\%$
  * $\text{Decreasing}$: Period-over-period percentage change $< -3.0\%$
  * $\text{Stable}$: Percentage change between $[-3.0\%, +3.0\%]$
* **Source**: `trend_analysis.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Daily / Weekly / Monthly
* **Example**: Monthly Revenue -> Decreasing (-33.09%)
* **Caveat**: Configurable threshold; does not project future trajectories.

### Statistical Anomaly Status
* **Definition**: Flag identifying daily values lying beyond historical quartile boundaries.
* **Formula**:
  * $\text{IQR} = Q_{75} - Q_{25}$
  * $\text{Upper Bound} = Q_{75} + 1.5 \times \text{IQR}$
  * $\text{Lower Bound} = Q_{25} - 1.5 \times \text{IQR}$
  * Status: `unusually_high` if $\text{value} > \text{Upper Bound}$, `unusually_low` if $\text{value} < \text{Lower Bound}$, else `normal`.
* **Source**: `anomaly_analysis.parquet`
* **Aggregation Level**: Daily
* **Example**: 2026-08-15 -> Status: `unusually_high`
* **Caveat**: Non-parametric historical filter; does not diagnose root cause.
