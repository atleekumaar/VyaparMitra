**Project:** VyaparMitra (MerchantOS) — Intelligence-led Operating System for Indian Retail Merchants  
**Website / Live App:** https://vyaparmitra-xdy9.onrender.com  
**Tone:** `polished` meets `default` (Energetic, clean, high-conversion fintech style)  
**Format:** 1920×1080 Landscape, 30 fps

## The Angle & Hook
- **Angle:** Transforming 60M+ traditional Kirana stores from manual diaries and broken bahi-khatas into an ultra-fast, AI-guided retail machine.
- **Hook:** "Still running your retail store on manual notebooks?" → Flash cut to instant 3-second smart billing and voice soundbox confirmation.
- **Punchline:** "Understand → Decide → Act. Dukaano ke liye smart AI OS."
