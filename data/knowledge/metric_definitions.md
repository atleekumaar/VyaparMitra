# VyaparMitra Metric Definitions (मापदंड परिभाषाएं)

Comprehensive definitions of analytical, predictive, and decision metrics used throughout VyaparMitra.

## Analytical Metrics (Phase 2)
- **Daily Revenue**: Total sum of net transaction amounts on a specific calendar date.
- **Order Count**: Total distinct bills/receipts generated.
- **RFM Score**:
  - `Recency`: Days elapsed since the customer's most recent completed order.
  - `Frequency`: Total transaction orders recorded for that customer over history.
  - `Monetary`: Total gross rupees spent by the customer over history.
- **Repeat Customer Rate**: Percentage of customer base with more than 1 completed purchase.

## Predictive Metrics (Phase 3)
- **7-Day Revenue Forecast**: Predicted cumulative revenue over the upcoming 7 calendar days generated by the Phase 3 Sales Model (`RandomForestRegressor`).
- **Product Demand Forecast**: Estimated units expected to sell per product SKU over the next 7 and 30 days (`HistGradientBoostingRegressor_Demand`).
- **Customer Churn Risk Probability**: Probability score from 0.0 to 1.0 indicating likelihood of 30-day inactivity (`RuleBasedChurnBaseline`).
- **Business Trend Direction**: Categorical classification (`INCREASING`, `STABLE`, `DECREASING`) comparing forward 7-day expected revenue against backward 7-day baseline.
- **WAPE (Weighted Absolute Percentage Error)**:
  $$\text{WAPE} = \frac{\sum |y - \hat{y}|}{\sum y} \times 100\%$$
  The primary metric used to evaluate forecast error. Lower is better.

## Decision Metrics (Phase 4)
- **Priority Score (0.0 to 1.0)**: Composite index ranking actions by $(\text{Impact} \times \text{Confidence} \times \text{Urgency})^{1/3}$.
- **Priority Bands**:
  - `CRITICAL` ($\ge 0.79$): Urgent intervention required immediately.
  - `HIGH` ($0.70 - 0.78$): High business impact, core priority.
  - `MEDIUM` ($0.55 - 0.69$): Valuable opportunity, secondary priority.
  - `LOW` ($< 0.55$): Operational monitoring or clearance.
- **Association Lift**: How many times more likely a customer is to buy Product B given they bought Product A compared to random chance. Lift $> 1.0$ indicates positive correlation.
